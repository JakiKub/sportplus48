From October 10th to March 26th, the world will unite for the 48 Sport Challenge - an innovative sport initiative designed to inspire milions across Europe and beyond.

For 48 days in autumn and 48 days in winter, participants will push their limits, collect kilometers and celebrate every form of movement - from running and cycling to team sports and individual training. It is more than a challenge: it is a movement that blends competition, integration and joy of being active.

Together, let's cover as many kilometers as possible. In 48 days.
